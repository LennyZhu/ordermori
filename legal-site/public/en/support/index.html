How do I create my first order?

Save your shop name in My Shop, then choose New order from Orders. Enter a buyer identifier, add items manually or from Saved products, check prices, quantities, delivery, the fixed discount and total, then choose Save order. A saved order must have a total greater than zero. Business orders use HKD.

Unfinished input can be saved as a draft; the app currently keeps one active draft at a time. Selected products are copied into the order, so later catalog changes or deletion do not automatically rewrite saved orders.

How does the 15-order Free allowance work?

Free includes up to 15 saved orders per Hong Kong calendar month, calculated in Asia/Hong_Kong. A new allowance starts on the first day; unused slots do not roll over. Only a successful creation uses a slot once. Draft saves and edits do not use another slot; cancellation, refund or deletion does not return one.

After the allowance is used, history, drafts, saved products, eligible notices and backups remain available. Restoring a backup retains known monthly usage and is not an allowance-reset feature. Correct your device clock if it has moved backwards.

What does OrderMori Pro include?

Pro is an Apple non-consumable, one-time purchase for unlimited saved orders and the option to remove Made with OrderMori from new payment-notice PNGs. There is no monthly fee or automatic renewal. Normal purchases have no time limit, except for cases such as Apple-confirmed refunds or revocations. Apple’s local purchase screen shows the actual price and currency.

Saved products, both bilingual templates, payment settings, sharing, Photos export and backups are already Free features. Upgrading does not redraw historical PNGs. Open My Shop → OrderMori Pro to view the product and purchase.

How do I restore an Apple purchase?

Use the Apple purchase account that originally bought Pro, then open My Shop → OrderMori Pro → Restore Purchases. Stay online and complete any Apple prompts so the app can check current valid entitlements. Pending approval, cancellation or incomplete verification does not unlock Pro early.

An eligible purchase can be restored on another device, but that does not transfer orders. Import a business backup separately; a business backup cannot grant Pro. If a purchase is still missing, check your Apple purchase history or contact support without sending your Apple password.

How do I back up and restore?

Open My Shop → Backup & restore → Create backup. Review the unencrypted-backup warning and summary, then use the system sheet to choose Save to Files and a trusted location. Closing the sheet does not confirm external saving; check that the file exists in Files.

Choose Restore from backup and select an .ordermori.zip or compatible legacy .ordermori file directly, without unzipping or renaming it. The app validates it and shows a summary first. Only your explicit confirmation replaces the current shop, products and orders; data is not merged. Back up the current data separately first and keep the app open during restoration.

Backups are not encrypted and may contain buyer and payment details and images. Restrict access and do not email them to support. The current limits are 256 MB and 512 files. Restoration retains known quota usage and cannot restore Apple purchase rights; language remains a device preference.

How do I share or save a payment notice?

Open Payment notice from an eligible saved order, choose Editorial Minimal or Soft Korean and the notice language, generate and save the PNG, then choose Share image or Save to Photos. Both templates and both notice languages are Free. A notice language can differ from the app language.

Allow iOS to add a photo when prompted on first save; permissions can later be managed in Settings. You choose the app and recipient in the iOS share sheet; there is no automatic WhatsApp sending. Notices for paid, refunded or cancelled orders, or outdated snapshots, may remain available only for historical viewing rather than export as a new payment request.

Who provides FPS and PayMe payment images?

The merchant obtains and imports images from official bank, FPS or PayMe tools, and checks the source, recipient and whether the code contains a fixed amount. The app does not generate payment QR codes or certify an arbitrary uploaded image as official. FPS supports qualifying text identifiers or official images; PayMe uses imported official images; bank transfer uses bank, account-holder and account details.

Incomplete settings can be saved, but incomplete or unconfirmed details cannot serve as an effective default or formal notice payment method. Changing shop payment settings does not silently overwrite a saved order’s payment snapshot.

Does the app automatically confirm payment?

No. A payment notice requests payment; it is not a receipt or proof that funds arrived. Check your bank or payment provider before manually marking an order as paid. Amounts become permanently locked on the first payment confirmation, even if an accidental paid mark is later corrected. Order, payment and fulfillment states are tracked separately.

How do I manage or delete local data?

You can delete saved products and local PNGs, or replace and remove payment-image settings. Cancelling an order is not deletion. Historical orders may still reference old payment originals, and restoration may retain old media. There is no one-tap erase-all-business-data feature.

If you need to remove all local app data, save anything you still need first, then use Delete App in iOS rather than Offload App, which can retain documents. Manage Photos, system backups, Files or iCloud Drive files and recipients’ copies separately. The developer cannot remotely retrieve or erase local orders. See the Privacy Policy.
