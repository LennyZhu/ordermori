What this policy covers

This policy covers the OrderMori 1.0 iOS app and the official OrderMori support website. OrderMori is a local order and payment-notice tool for small shops. It does not require an OrderMori account or provide a developer-operated order-sync server. Contact the developer at lenny996@163.com.

This policy distinguishes on-device processing, exports you initiate, Apple purchases, support email and website visits. A payment notice is not a paid receipt. The app does not automatically confirm bank, FPS or PayMe payments.

Information kept on your device

The app uses local SQLite databases and app files for your shop name and description, saved product names and prices, buyer identifiers, order items, quantities, amounts, delivery, discounts, notes, drafts, status history, and order-creation and monthly-quota records. Your language preference is stored in a separate device-settings database. Enter only information you are authorized to use and need for an order.

Payment details can include an FPS ID, linked phone number or email address, bank name, account-holder name, bank account number, merchant confirmations and official payment images you import. Generated PNGs and their template, language, snapshots, file sizes and SHA-256 integrity metadata are also stored locally. Hashes check integrity; they do not encrypt the data.

Automatic transmission and app components

The reviewed 1.0 app has no flow that automatically uploads orders, buyer details, payment images or PNGs to a developer server, and includes no advertising, usage-analytics or third-party crash-reporting service. This does not mean information can never leave your device: Apple store services, recipients you choose, file providers, support email and website hosting involve different processing.

The app uses Expo and React Native components for local databases, files, image selection, PNGs, system sharing, Photos and language. expo-iap connects to Apple StoreKit. RevenueCat, hosted IAPKit validation and developer-operated purchase-validation backends are not integrated. Development builds have stage and error-code logs; the relevant development logs and detailed IAP logs are disabled by default in release builds, with no automatic diagnostic-upload service configured. iOS, Apple or device providers may still handle system diagnostics according to your settings.

Photo and file access

You open the system image picker to import an official payment PNG or JPEG obtained from your provider. The app reads the selected file, checks its format and dimensions, and copies its original content into persistent app storage rather than relying on the picker cache. This feature does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Only when you choose “Save to Photos” does the app request the iOS permission needed to add the selected PNG to your photo library. Backup import uses the system file picker to read a file you select. You can refuse or manage permissions in iOS Settings; the corresponding feature may then be unavailable.

Sharing and unencrypted backups

You can send a payment-notice PNG to an app or recipient you select through the iOS share sheet, or save it to Photos. How recipients, receiving apps and photo-sync services retain or process copies depends on their terms, policies and your settings. Closing the share sheet does not prove delivery and never marks an order as paid.

A full backup is an unencrypted ZIP, normally ending in .ordermori.zip. It includes shop, product, order, draft, status, quota and payment-snapshot data, associated payment images and historical PNGs. Compatible legacy .ordermori files can also be imported. Anyone who obtains a backup may be able to read buyer and payment details. SHA-256 helps detect damage; it does not prove a backup is trustworthy.

You choose whether to save a backup to Files, iCloud Drive or another location. The app does not automatically upload business backups to the developer. Use a trusted location, restrict access and manage external copies yourself. Device-level iCloud or computer backups depend separately on Apple and your system settings; they are not an OrderMori order-sync service.

Apple purchases and Pro entitlements

OrderMori Pro is an Apple non-consumable, one-time in-app purchase, with no subscription renewal or normal expiry date. An Apple-confirmed refund or revocation can invalidate it. Apple provides the price and currency for your App Store region, separately from business orders recorded in HKD.

StoreKit and expo-iap read product information, product and transaction identifiers, transaction and revocation status, store environment and transaction data needed for signature verification on your device to display pricing, verify, finish and restore purchases. OrderMori does not ask you to give it your Apple password or payment-card details. Apple handles purchase-account and payment information under its own policy.

Only Apple-verified current entitlements unlock Pro. A success callback or business-backup setting is not proof of purchase. Purchase credentials are not included in business backups or sent to a developer-operated validation server. Verified entitlements can reasonably continue offline using StoreKit’s local transaction data, but a new refund or revocation may not be known immediately while offline. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Retention, deletion and your control

You can edit data when the app allows it, delete saved products, remove local PNGs and replace or remove payment-image settings. Cancelling or refunding an order does not delete its history. Old payment-image originals may remain to preserve historical order snapshots; safe restoration may also retain old media. There is currently no one-tap command to erase all business data or delete every saved order.

To remove the app’s local business data, first save anything you still need, then choose Delete App in iOS. “Offload App” can retain documents and data and is different. System backups, images saved to Photos, Files or iCloud Drive backups and recipients’ copies must be managed separately. Removing an app PNG does not remove its Photos copy, and deleting the app does not cancel an Apple purchase.

We do not have a server that can remotely retrieve or erase your local orders. You can email us to request access, correction or deletion of support correspondence; we will handle verifiable requests as applicable. Do not send your full order database or payment credentials merely to make a request.

Support email

When you contact lenny996@163.com, the developer and email-service providers process your email address, message, attachments and correspondence to reply, investigate the issue you raise and handle reasonable requests. Necessary correspondence is retained for support needs and applicable retention requirements, with information no longer needed reviewed; immediate automatic deletion is not promised.

Redact buyer names, contact details, FPS or bank details, payment QR codes and backup contents first. Never send Apple passwords, verification codes or payment-card details. If diagnostics are needed, we will explain the minimum information required; we do not automatically obtain your app contents.

Website hosting and access data

This website contains static HTML, CSS and local images, with no contact form, analytics SDK, advertising script or tracking cookie added by us. Visiting a support or privacy page does not give the website access to orders inside your app.

When you use a Vercel-hosted deployment, Vercel may process IP addresses, browser or device information, request paths, times, referrers and technical logs to deliver pages, provide security, prevent abuse and operate its service. Processing may occur outside your location. Actual processing and retention depend on deployment settings and the Vercel Privacy Notice. We do not promise that the website produces no access logs or that we control all provider records. The site uses system fonts rather than contacting an external font service.

Updates and contact

This policy takes effect on 30 October 2026 and covers the 1.0 features described here. We will update this page and its date when features or processing change, with appropriate notice for material changes. This policy does not guarantee absolute security of devices, files or communications.

For privacy or data-control questions, contact the OrderMori developer at lenny996@163.com. For practical help, see Support.
